How LoopCycle Works

A simple 3-step circular flow designed for zero waste and ironclad trust.

01

List or Discover

Upload items you no longer need or find items listed by students and neighbors around you.

02

Choose your Loop

Buy via Escrow, rent short-term, or barter textbook-for-textbook with zero cash exchanged.

03

Safe Handover

Verify the item in person. Confirm delivery to release payment and award +10 Trust Score to the seller.
